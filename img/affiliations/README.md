# Affiliation artwork

`uic-official.webp` is the University of Illinois Chicago circle mark, imported
from UIC's official visual identity guide on 2026-11-14:

- Guide: https://brand.uic.edu/visual-identity/logos/
- Source: https://brand.uic.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1284/2025/03/CAMP.CIRC600x365.png

Only transparent exterior padding was removed and the image was resized to
256px for the site's small logo slots. Colors, lettering, and proportions were
preserved. The organization name appears alongside the mark in the directory.

The former `uic.png` and `uic.webp` are actually the Urbana-Champaign Block I.
They remain available for historical URLs but must not identify UIC.
