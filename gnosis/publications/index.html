Featured & award-winning

Best Paper Award (First Prize)

DLIO: A Data-Centric Benchmark for Scientific Deep Learning Applications

The 2021 IEEE/ACM International Symposium in Cluster, Cloud, and Internet Computing (CCGrid'21), May 17 - 20, 2021 Best paper award2021

H. Devarajan, H. Zheng, A. Kougkas, X.-H. Sun, V. Vishwanath

DLIO is a benchmark suite that emulates the I/O behavior of scientific deep learning workloads, based on profiling on Argonne's Theta supercomputer. It helps application developers and system architects identify I/O bottlenecks and guide optimizations that reduce training time.

Karsten Schwan Best Paper Award

LABIOS: A Distributed Label-Based I/O System

The 28th International Symposium on High-Performance Parallel and Distributed Computing (HPDC'19), Phoenix, USA 2019. pp. 13-24. Karsten Schwan Best Paper Award2019

A. Kougkas, H. Devarajan, J. Lofstead, X.-H. Sun

LABIOS uses a label-based data representation and asynchronous I/O to support conflicting scientific and Big Data workloads within a single distributed storage system. It combines heterogeneous storage resources, storage elasticity, and data provisioning for in-situ analytics.
