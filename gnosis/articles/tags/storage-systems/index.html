ChronoLog's year: from 2.5 to 3.5

Software Releases · November 12, 2026 · 5 min read
Gnosis Research Center

Between April 2 and November 12, the ChronoLog team published eleven releases, from 2.5.0 to 3.5.0. Over those seven months, GRC's distributed log store gained SQL, publish-subscribe and agent interfaces. It also learned to keep every event it acknowledges, even through a crash, and moved to a current HPC software stack. This is a summary of the year, release by release.
