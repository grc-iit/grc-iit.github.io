Open to final-year undergraduates, bachelor's degree holders with no prior graduate enrollment, qualifying joint bachelor's-master's students, and first-year students in their first graduate degree program with less than one academic year completed.

NERSC Summer Internships, Lawrence Berkeley National Lab

internship
Paid internship (stipend varies by project)
DeadlineRolling (projects posted Jan–late Spring 2026)
DurationSummer 2026 (~10–12 weeks, up to 6 months for some projects)
RequiresApply directly to project mentors; open to graduate students and undergraduate juniors/seniors
HPCAI for ScienceGPU PerformanceQuantum ComputingContainers & WorkflowsLoad BalancingData Analytics

NERSC (National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center) at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab is a DOE computing facility. Summer 2026 offers 15+ projects spanning quantum computing, agentic AI for HPC operations, GPU performance modeling, scientific AI workload benchmarking, container engineering, load balancing, and metascheduling. Projects of particular relevance to GRC students include: "Agentic AI for HPC Operations and Scientific AI Infrastructure", "Performance Benchmarking of Scientific AI Workloads for Next-Gen HPC Systems", "GPU Performance Modeling", "Improving AMReX Load Balancing Algorithms", "Spin Workload Analysis" (storage I/O focus), and "Container Images for the NERSC Programming Environment". Also part of the Computing Sciences Area summer program at LBNL.

How to Pursue These Opportunities

  1. Talk to your advisor about which opportunities align with your research direction and timeline.
  2. Most require faculty nomination: your advisor must submit on your behalf. Discuss well in advance.
  3. Start early: most applications take 2 to 4 weeks to prepare. A rushed application rarely wins.
  4. A strong research statement is the most important component. Invest time in articulating your vision.
  5. Publications matter: having peer-reviewed papers strengthens applications.
