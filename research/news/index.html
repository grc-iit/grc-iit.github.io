GRC at SC26 (opens in a new tab)

GRC researchers will present eleven accepted works at SC26 from November 16 to 19, 2026, and Anthony Kougkas will co-lead a Birds of a Feather session. All times are CST. On Monday, November 16, at PDSW'26, the 11th International Parallel Data Systems Workshop (room W175bc), Fernandes et al. will present GPUH5, a GPU-first I/O library for scientific data (10:50 to 11:10am); Yildirim et al. will present GDSight, per-operation cross-layer attribution for GPUDirect Storage (11:30 to 11:50am); Xu et al. will give a five-minute talk on trigger-gated multimodal LLM agents for in situ simulation monitoring (12:15 to 12:20pm); and Teves et al. will give a five-minute talk on type-safe, dynamically defined metadata for scientific applications (4:45 to 4:50pm). The same morning, at ProTools'26, the 8th Workshop on Programming and Performance Visualization Tools (room W179b), Devarajan et al. will present Orion (10:30 to 10:54am) and DataCrumbs (10:54 to 11:18am), both with GRC coauthors. On Tuesday, November 17, four research posters will be on display in North Pre-Function from 10:00am to 5:00pm: ToolFuse and StageOnce by Yildirim et al., I/O observability extended to explainability by Rajesh et al., and GNNs Beyond HBM by Pawar et al. On Wednesday, November 18, Xu et al. will present the paper "Trigger, Render, Reason: Multimodal LLM Agents for Science-Aware Scientific Simulation Monitoring" in the session Papers in Conversation: Scientific AI at Extreme Scale (4:30 to 5:00pm, room W181abc). On Thursday, November 19, the Birds of a Feather session "Trustworthy Data at Scale: HDF5 in the Age of AI-Driven Science" will meet from 12:15 to 1:15pm in room W185d, led by Michael Breitenfeld of The HDF Group with Elena Pourmal, Suren Byna, Quincey Koziol, and Anthony Kougkas.

ChronoLog 3.2 Keeps Every Chunk Until It Is Archived (opens in a new tab)

ChronoLog 3.2.0 adds durable chunk retention. A ChronoKeeper now holds each sealed chunk until ChronoGrapher confirms it written to the HDF5 archive, and a replay reads events that are not yet archived directly from the keepers. The client protocol version changes, so clients built against 3.1 must be rebuilt. The release follows 3.1.0 and 3.1.1 in September 2026.

Four GRC Papers Presented at eScience 2026 (opens in a new tab)

Four GRC papers were presented in the main track of the 22nd IEEE International Conference on eScience, held in Naples, Italy, from September 28 to October 2, 2026. Bateman et al. describe the Agent Error Simulator, a fault-injection benchmark for multi-agent LLM workflows. Pawar et al. describe Acropolis, a semantic search system that indexes a tiered filesystem for LLM agents. Yildirim et al. describe AgentStage, which stages data while an agent is still reasoning. Xu et al. describe derived quantity operators for in situ analysis inside high-level I/O libraries.

  • PaperAgent Error Simulator: Fault Injection for Testing Recovery in Agentic Workflows
  • PaperAcropolis: A Portable, AI-Driven Semantic Search System for HPC
  • PaperAgentStage: Exploiting LLM Thinking for Data Staging in Scientific Agents
  • PaperRich I/O-centric Derived Quantity Operators for Advanced In Situ Processing

Nov 172025

GRC Congratulates Meng Tang on Cloud Storage Research at PDSW'25

The Gnosis Research Center congratulates Meng Tang for the publication of "Quantifying AWS S3 I/O Performance Boundaries Using the Roofline Model" at the International Parallel Data Systems Workshop (PDSW 2025). This work extends I/O roofline modeling to characterize performance boundaries across three S3 integration approaches, demonstrating up to 6.4x bandwidth differences between access methods. The analysis is intended to guide the selection of S3 integration strategies for hybrid cloud environments.
